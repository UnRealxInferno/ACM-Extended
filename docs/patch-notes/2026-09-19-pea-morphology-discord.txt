ACM Extended 1.2.1-rc1 | PEA morphology

- Default and obstructive PEA now use a narrow, sinus-like P-QRS-T tracing.
- The existing wide PEA tracing is reserved for severe transfusion burden not covered by calcium.
- Calcium coverage can narrow the tracing while an unresolved obstructive cause keeps the patient in PEA.
- The monitor detects morphology changes without resetting its electrical beat clock.
- Both forms remain pulseless and nonshockable.

This uses ACME's existing transfusion/calcium burden as a gameplay proxy. It does not add a measured potassium level or change arrest-entry/ROSC logic. At an 80 BPM resting baseline, wide PEA requires more than 2 L of uncovered burden.
